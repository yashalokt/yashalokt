    The shared-charger problem

    Every society with EVs hits the same three questions.

    "Is any charger free right now?"

    Residents walk down to the basement to check, or post in the group and wait for replies.

    "Whose car is still plugged in at 100%?"

    Fully charged cars block the bay for hours while the next person waits upstairs.

    "Who used how many units?"

    The facility team copies meter readings into a spreadsheet and splits the bill by hand.

    How it works

    From "anyone free?" to plugged in, without a single message.

    1. Join the queue

      Open the app, pick how long you need, and tap Join. You see your place in line and an estimated start time.

    2. Get your turn

      When a bay frees up, EVNest notifies the next flat and holds the charger for 10 minutes.

    3. Plug in and charge

      Scan the bay's QR code to start. Track progress, units drawn and cost live on your phone.

    4. Unplug and release

      Get a reminder when your session ends. The bay goes straight to the next person in line.

    Features

    Built for a few chargers shared by many flats.

    Fair queue

    First come, first served across all bays, with estimated start times that update as sessions end early or run long.

    Plug-in hold

    The next flat gets a short window to plug in. Miss it and the bay passes on, so nobody waits on a no-show.

    Default hold: 10 min

    Idle alerts

    Owners get a nudge when the battery is full. An optional idle fee kicks in if the car keeps sitting on the charger.

    e.g. ₹2 per idle minute after 15 min

    Per-flat billing

    Every session is metered in kWh and priced at the society's rate. Totals export straight into the maintenance bill.

    Load management

    Keeps total draw within the society's sanctioned load, so chargers never trip the building's main supply.

    e.g. 3 bays × 7.4 kW = 22.2 kW cap

    Overnight slots

    Book a bay for the off-peak window ahead of time, with cheaper night tariffs where your DISCOM offers time-of-day rates.
